Home is renamed Your day: the digest, then your tasks in two lists. Each row shows what it is, the state it is in, and its target date. No filters, sorting controls or counts beyond the list heading. What Review, Ingest and drift hold stays on their own tabs, with the counts on the rail.

Tickets: the team's ticket files assigned to you (tickets/ATT-014.md, Wright's format), open ones only. A row is the id, the title over the repo it touches and how many of your tasks link to it, the state as the ticket file states it, and the target date. The row opens the ticket in Files. The state is changed in the ticket. A team with no tickets folder shows no Tickets list.

Other: tasks, todos and notes, all one thing: a title, and optionally a target date, a description, a repeat, and a link to a ticket, a file or a repo. The state is open or done, set with the checkbox; a done item stays greyed until the end of the day, then leaves the list. A task a teammate sent through the team inbox shows Accept ▾ (Accept, Not mine, Reply) until it is accepted. Ordered by target date, then the ones without one. A task linked to a ticket shows the ticket id.
