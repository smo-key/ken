# Work

Release notes, release history and incidents. Not here: tickets, the Ideas list and escalations → the team repo; what is planned → [[Roadmap]]; what is built now → [[Feature Status]]; a new version of a dependency → its page on [[Platform]].

## Belongs Here

A record of what shipped and what went wrong, kept as history. A release's notes are written from each merged pull request's release-notes line, under Unreleased until the version is released. An incident page is written from `Templates/Incident.md`; the rule it produces goes to [[Rules]] and carries the incident with it.
