# Research

What past sessions found, each dated, and the Ingestion inbox. Not here: what is true now → [[Current]]; how a dependency behaves, once checked → [[Platform]]; a ruling → the decisions log.

[[#Folders]] · [[#Belongs Here]]

## Folders

| folder | holds |
|---|---|
| `Findings/` | one dated page per question a session or spike answered · written from `Templates/Finding.md` |
| `Ingestion/` | the inbox for meetings, recordings, documents and notes · [[Ingestion]] |

## Belongs Here

Evidence at the time it was written, never current state: a Research page is cited with its date and never read as the rules. What still holds once checked moves to the page that owns it, with the finding as a source. The drift checks report Research in a group of its own, because its citations are expected to go stale.
