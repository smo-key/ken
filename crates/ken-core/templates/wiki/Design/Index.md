## Belongs Here

A design note says what problem it answers, what was chosen, what was chosen against and why, and which rulings it rests on. It is written from `Templates/Design-Note.md`. A note cites rulings by their D-nnn and never copies them, because the decisions log is the record and a copy drifts from it.
