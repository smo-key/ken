How we work: the lifecycle, where to look in what order, and the rules. It is the only section people must follow; every other section describes what exists. ## Belongs Here

Anything that constrains how work is done, whatever the work is. A page belongs here if breaking it would be a process failure rather than a wrong answer. Every rule is its own page, so its backlinks show which work used it, and every rule also has a short form in the brief, `CLAUDE.md`. A change here becomes a ticket.
