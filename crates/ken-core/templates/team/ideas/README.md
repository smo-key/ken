# ideas/

The Ideas list, shown on Intake's Ideas tab. One file per rough idea, written
from `IDEA.md`. An idea is not work until a person takes it to Brainstorm,
Define or Direct and a brainstorm or a ticket comes out. A person may take one
idea, or several selected together. The backlog is the Board's ranked list of
confirmed tickets, never this list.

Anyone adds an idea from the one-line box on the Ideas tab, or by telling Wright
anywhere. When a person talks to Wright on the Ideas tab, Wright writes each
idea in the person's words. It may also add ideas of its own, marked as raised
by Wright. Rows written from an Ingested note or by the standing sweep are
marked as raised by Wright too. Every row Wright raises shows grey until a
person has seen it. The options a brainstorm did not choose come back here as rows.

| status | means |
|---|---|
| `open` | on the list |
| `taken` | taken to Brainstorm, Define or Direct · `went_to` says where |
| `done` | a merged ticket covered it · the standing sweep sets it |
| `dropped` | a person dropped it · `reason` says why |
| `cold` | nobody touched it for thirty days · the standing sweep marks it cold and folds it away · one click brings it back |
